What to do next

    ⚠️ Do not click links or open attachments just to “test” them. If you are unsure, verify the message independently using the organisation’s official website, app, or a known phone number.

    Your privacy

    Everything you paste is analysed entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript. Your email text is never uploaded to a server, sent to an AI provider, or shared with any analytics or tracking service. There are no cookies, no accounts, and no telemetry. You can even save this page and run it fully offline.

    How the score works

    The analyser checks your email against a set of named warning signs (urgent language, password requests, suspicious links, attachment red flags, and more). Each warning sign is worth a defined number of points based on how serious it is. The points are added up and capped at 100, and the result is placed into one of three bands:

    • Low Risk — 0–24 points: few or no warning signs.
    • Suspicious — 25–59 points: several warning signs worth investigating.
    • High Risk — 60–100 points: many strong warning signs. Treat with caution.

    Every detected warning sign is listed with its points, so the number is never a mystery.
